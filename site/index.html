Who we are

Building Ghana’s healthcare capacity, one facility at a time.

Founded in 2008, Flokefama is a Ghana Club 100 company and one of the country’s most trusted medical equipment suppliers. We represent world-leading manufacturers and serve public and private facilities nationwide.

Our 2030 vision: to manufacture reagents and hospital disposables here in Ghana, and to be listed on the Ghana Stock Exchange.
