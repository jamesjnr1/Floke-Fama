About Us

FLOKEFAMA LIMITED founded in 2008, is a registered company specializing , in delivering world-class healthcare equipment across Ghana and West Africa. With a strong presence in both the private and public sectors, we
represent diverse medical equipment manufacturers, bringing innovative technology to our clients and contributing significantly to the development
of healthcare in the markets we serve.

Our Mission

What we stand for

FLOKEFAMA is recognized as one of the most reputable and trusted medical
equipment suppliers in Ghana.

Our Mission

To provide world class medical solutions to all medical facilities and laboratories in the West African sub
region.

Our
Vision

To have a localized industrial complex which produces all medical equipments and reagents locally and to be listed on the Ghana Stock Exchange by 2030

Our
Aim

To have a production plant in Ghana to produce our reagents and some of our hospital disposables locally to boost the economy and create meaningful well paying jobs.

Our Core Values

At Flokefama Limited, our success is built on a strong foundation of core values that guide every aspect of our operations. These principles define who we are, how we work, and the impact we strive to make in the healthcare industry.

HONESTY

Being transparent and truthful in all business dealings, ensuring clear communication and setting realistic expectations. We build trust through reliability, consistently delivering what we promise.

INTEGRITY

Upholding strong ethical standards and maintaining consistency in quality and service. We demonstrate timeliness by ensuring prompt delivery, meeting deadlines, and responding swiftly to customer needs.

INNOVATION

Continuously improving and embracing new ideas, technologies, and solutions to enhance efficiency and meet evolving needs. Our commitment to innovation drives unmatched customer service, providing clients with cutting-edge solutions and exceptional support.

RESPECT

Valuing people by fostering professionalism, inclusivity, and strong relationships. We uphold unmatched customer service by treating every client, partner, and team member with care, attentiveness, and responsiveness.
